# Image-generation prompt set

These ten source-photo prompts were run with OpenAI's built-in image generation
tool. Exact marketing text was added later by the deterministic renderer rather
than generated inside the photographs.

## 1. Late-night ceiling

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: TikTok UGC slideshow background, vertical portrait
Primary request: candid first-person iPhone photo taken in bed at 1:43 a.m., looking up toward a dim bedroom ceiling with a small warm bedside lamp glow, edge of rumpled duvet visible, phone-camera imperfection
Scene/backdrop: modest contemporary bedroom at night, lived-in but tidy enough, no people visible
Style/medium: photorealistic natural smartphone photography, authentic UGC camera-roll look
Composition/framing: 9:16 vertical, generous dark negative space in the upper-middle for later text overlay, duvet and a sliver of bedside table at the lower edge
Lighting/mood: very low warm practical light, reflective and slightly restless, realistic digital noise
Materials/textures: wrinkled cotton bedding, natural wall texture, ordinary charging cable
Constraints: no text, no logos, no watermark, no readable phone screen, no staged commercial polish, no face, no hands
```
