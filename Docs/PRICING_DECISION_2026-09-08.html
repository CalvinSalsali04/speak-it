Speak It pricing decision

For Calvin • 8 September 2026 • US dollar reference prices

Decision

Keep 10 lifetime free captures, $2.99 monthly, and the $14.99 annual launch price against a $29.99 standard annual price. Do not introduce weekly billing or a second auto-renewing trial at launch. This preserves a low-commitment way to experience the complete app. Ten captures is a starting hypothesis, not a proven optimum.

The source configuration already contained these prices. Both StoreKit introductory offers are null. The launch flag is enabled and ends 22 October 2026 at 04:00 UTC. These are repository facts, not confirmation of live App Store Connect billing.

Why this fits the product

Speak It demonstrates its core value in a successful capture. An allowance with no expiry lets occasional users try it at their own pace without authorizing a future charge. A heavy user can exhaust ten captures in a day, so this may be insufficient to test reminders and sustained habits. That is the strongest reason to test a seven-day trial later; it is not inherently too generous to offer both.

Monthly costs $35.88 across twelve payments. Standard annual saves $5.89 (16.4%); launch annual saves $20.89 (58.2%) against monthly payments. The advertised 50% refers instead to $29.99 standard annual, rounded from 50.02%. Launch annual is approximately $1.25 per month but must prominently say $14.99 billed yearly. Even $0.99 weekly costs $51.48 across 52 charges. Weekly adds frequent renewal decisions and an annualized expense poorly aligned with the requested affordable, predictable experience. These are arithmetic and product judgments, not conversion forecasts.

Evidence and counterevidence

RevenueCat's 2026 report uses 2025 performance from more than 115,000 integrated apps. Its retention analysis favors longer billing durations; trial-to-paid medians are 25.5% for trials of four days or less, 37.4% for five to nine days, and 42.5% for 17–32 days. These are observational benchmarks across different products and audiences. They do not prove changing this app's plan or trial duration will improve retention or revenue. Trial-to-paid is not install-to-paid. RevenueCat, State of Subscription Apps 2026.

Bear's official current FAQ lists $2.99 monthly and $29.99 yearly, with a seven-day trial on both. It is a useful small productivity-app anchor and a counterexample to the idea that productivity apps should never offer trials. Its feature gates differ from Speak It's capture allowance, so the same conversion result cannot be assumed. Bear, Features and price of Bear Pro.

Apple supports free trials through introductory offers, with one introductory offer per subscription group. Trial duration and the subsequent charge must be clearly disclosed. If a trial is added later, fetch its terms and eligibility from StoreKit; do not merely relabel a purchase button. Apple, Auto-renewable subscriptions; Apple, Set up introductory offers.

Apple allows prices to be preserved for existing subscribers when increasing the price for new subscribers. This requires App Store Connect configuration; local code cannot enforce it. Apple, Manage pricing for auto-renewable subscriptions.

Yesterday's research overreached by describing category-level trial LTV differences as trials destroying value and suggesting that changing the App Store category should determine trial policy. Those conclusions are withdrawn. Category selection should accurately describe the product; the trial decision should follow this app's users and experiments.

Implementation and live setup

Retained the existing prices, product IDs and ten-capture allowance. Clarified in-app copy: unused captures never expire and need no subscription. Added a price/currency check so annual launch headers, strike-throughs, accessibility text and offer terms only appear for the configured USD 14.99 price during the enabled sale window. Non-USD products still display StoreKit's localized price without claiming an unverified 50% comparison. Removed the unconditional promise that the rate stays forever, pending verified preservation settings.

Before releasing the promotion, verify monthly USD 2.99 and annual USD 14.99 in App Store Connect; schedule the genuine USD 29.99 standard annual price for October 22; preserve the lower price for existing annual subscribers if adopting the recommended continuing launch rate. Check Apple’s effective date/time by storefront against the app’s UTC cutoff. Keep introductory offers absent. Verify the Sandbox purchase sheet and renewal terms, including a non-US storefront. The website's annual launch flag is currently empty; activate its promotion only after live terms are confirmed. No live billing or website deployment was performed in this task. Local price matching cannot prove the future standard price exists.

What would change the decision

Collect content-free counts of successful first capture, reaching ten captures, paywall views, purchases, cancellations and refunds, plus retained use and support feedback. Review whether users reach the cap before they can experience reminders or a habit. If so, compare the unchanged allowance against allowance plus a seven-day eligible StoreKit trial, holding prices and acquisition mix constant. Evaluate net revenue per install at 60 days, paid conversion, retained use and refunds together; annual retention needs a longer follow-up. Do not choose based on trial starts alone. With little traffic, qualitative evidence is more useful than declaring a noisy A/B winner.

No acquisition costs, operating margins or app-specific conversion cohort were available. These prices are a reasonable launch baseline, not a demonstrated profit optimum.
