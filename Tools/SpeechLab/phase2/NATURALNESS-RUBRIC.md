# Naturalness rubric

Naturalness is judged from the utterance alone, before seeing parser behavior.
Reviewers record a countable integer rather than a prose-only impression.

| Score | Definition | Typical evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Sounds like spontaneous real speech | Natural contraction, pacing, reference, or self-repair without benchmark framing |
| 4 | Natural enough, mildly clean | A person could readily say it, but it is concise or carefully formed |
| 3 | Plausible but noticeably synthetic | Meaning is recoverable, but the construction or phenomenon combination feels authored |
| 2 | Awkward or benchmark-like | Strongly unnatural phrasing, mechanical slot recital, or implausible repair |
| 1 | Broken or implausible | Ungrammatical beyond realistic speech, contradictory, or not interpretable as intended |
