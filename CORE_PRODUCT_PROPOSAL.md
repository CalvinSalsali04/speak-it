# Core Product Proposal

## Product

Speak It is the fastest and most trustworthy place for an iPhone user to put a thought before it disappears.

> Speak naturally. Speak It saves, organizes, and resurfaces what matters.

The app separates capture from organization. A person records the thought immediately; Speak It preserves the original wording, structures it later, and resurfaces useful information at the right time.

## Product principles

1. Capture must be immediate.
2. The original thought must never be lost or silently replaced.
3. Saving is more important than organizing.
4. Minimalism should remove decisions.
5. Automation should remain quiet and editable.
6. Clarification happens after capture.
7. Trust and privacy outweigh novelty.
8. New features must improve capture, reliability, organization, resurfacing, or trust.

## Audience

The initial user is an iPhone-owning student or young professional who remembers tasks, errands, ideas, deadlines, and follow-ups throughout the day and wants less friction than a traditional task manager.

## Core loop

1. **Capture:** Save the complete statement.
2. **Understand:** Separate distinct thoughts.
3. **Organize:** Suggest type, category, timing, and confidence.
4. **Resurface:** Present relevant items in Today, Inbox, and a future briefing.

## MVP proof

The MVP succeeds when a user can activate Speak It, record one natural thought, receive immediate confirmation, and later find the correct information safely stored and usefully organized.
