How can I help?

Speak It is made by one person. Send what happened, what you expected, and your iPhone/iOS version, and you'll get a human reply.

Email Speak It support

My Pro purchase is missing

Open Speak It → Account & Settings → Speak It Pro, then choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Apple controls billing and entitlement history; restoring never charges you.

I have an offer or founder code

Open Speak It → Account & Settings → Speak It Pro → Redeem Code. Redemption is presented and verified by the App Store. The app does not contain hidden local unlock codes.

A referral reward has not appeared

Your friend must accept the invite, redeem the one-month App Store offer, and open Speak It so the signed transaction can be verified. Sharing, clicking, cancelling, or an ineligible redemption does not create a reward. When Apple verifies it, the reward appears under Give a month. Get a month. A cancelled reward claim can be tried again.

Voice capture is not transcribing

Check iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and Speech Recognition and allow Speak It. Try again in a quiet room without Bluetooth, then retry with AirPods if that is your normal setup. An interrupted capture can be recovered from Capture history.

A place reminder is not firing

Speak It shows the reason on the item itself, in Today under Needs review — it never leaves a place reminder looking armed when iOS is not watching it. The usual causes: Home or Work has not been set yet (Account & Settings → Places), location access is not set to Always, or Precise Location is off. iOS also caps how many places one app may watch at a time, and a place reminder cannot fire without notification permission. A reminder that names both a place and a time is kept but not scheduled, and asks you to choose one.

Back Tap or the Action Button does not start capture

Open Speak It → Capture anywhere and follow the guide for your device. iOS requires you to choose the Speak It Capture shortcut in system Settings; an app cannot assign Back Tap or the Action Button itself.

Privacy or deletion request

Your library is on your iPhone, with optional private iCloud Sync. Read the full Privacy Policy. For a referral-data request, email support from the address above and include the referral code if you still have it; never send a transaction receipt or Apple Account password.
