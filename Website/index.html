Common questions

Before you try it.

Why use this instead of Notes or Reminders?+

Speak It gives you one place to begin, whether you’re saying something to do or something to remember. Tasks go to Today; ideas and details go to Memory. You can speak first without choosing a list or folder.

What if it gets something wrong?+

Your original words are kept. In the app, open an item to edit its title, category, date, or reminder. Review anything unclear before relying on it.

Does it work without an internet connection?+

Your saved library, search, and task completion work offline. Speech uses Apple’s Speech framework and may require a connection. You can type a thought when speaking isn’t available.

What happens after the ten free captures?+

The free allowance is ten captures in total and does not renew. Pro enables more captures. Your saved items stay accessible if you don’t subscribe or later cancel.

Will it remind me when I get home?+

The app supports Home and Work place reminders after you set those places and grant the required location and notification permissions. The browser preview does not set reminders or request your location.

Will it work on my iPhone?+

Speak It works on iPhones running iOS 17 or later. You don’t need Apple Intelligence. Options such as the Action Button depend on your iPhone model.

Are my thoughts private?+

Your thoughts are stored on your device, with optional sync through your private iCloud container. Organizing happens on your iPhone. Speech recognition may use Apple’s services. Read the privacy policy.
