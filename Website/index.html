Where it starts

Seven ways in.

Every one starts a capture without you finding an icon first.

  • Lock Screen A widget on the wake screen
  • Action Button One press, iPhone 15 Pro and later
  • Back Tap Two taps on the back of the phone
  • Control Center A control you can put anywhere
  • Siri Hands free, through an App Intent
  • Share sheet Send text in from anything else
  • Live Activity A running capture stays on screen
  • Stays on your iPhone Thoughts, tasks, memories and places are stored on the device. There is no Speak It server holding them.
  • No account Nothing to sign up for. Turn on sync and it goes to your own private iCloud container, not to us.
  • Works offline Open it, search it and check things off with no signal at all. Only the speech-to-text may reach Apple, and only while you speak.
